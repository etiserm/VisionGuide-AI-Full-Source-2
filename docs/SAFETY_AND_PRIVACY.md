# Safety, privacy, and user feedback

## Safety boundary

VisionGuide is an experimental prototype. Detection and spoken output may be incomplete, incorrect, delayed, or unavailable. It is not a certified navigation or safety device. Never use it as the basis for crossing a street, avoiding hazards, or choosing a route. Keep using established mobility aids and skills.

Distance and step-range estimates in the mobile apps are approximate, depend on camera geometry and assumed object sizes, and may be wrong. Treat them only as experimental descriptions, not measurements.

## Camera and data handling

The Android and iOS project documentation says that camera analysis is performed locally and frames are not saved or uploaded by those apps. This describes the supplied app code and documentation; it is not an independent security audit. The Python prototype may access image files and camera input, and its behavior should be reviewed before use. Research/demo images included in the repository should be treated as project assets whose provenance and sharing rights the maintainers must confirm.

Do not commit personal data, credentials, private logs, or identifiable participant recordings. If collecting feedback, explain what is collected, why, who can access it, how long it is retained, and how a participant can withdraw. Obtain appropriate informed consent before collecting personal or sensitive information.

## Safe feedback sessions

Use a controlled indoor or otherwise pre-checked setting. Ask participants to assess clarity, timing, repetition, and comfort; never ask them to depend on the prototype for mobility. Stop if a participant is uncomfortable or the software behaves unstably. Obtain any additional permission required for minors through their legal guardian.

This guidance is not a legal consent form. The team must prepare a separate consent process appropriate to its location and study before conducting user research.
