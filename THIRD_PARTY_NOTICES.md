# Third-party notices and licensing status

The repository omits pretrained and exported model binaries, screenshots, and sample/research images because their redistribution rights have not been confirmed. Model exporter scripts may download upstream checkpoints; review their terms before running exports or redistributing generated artifacts.

The project team has not supplied a consolidated license review or a project license. Before distributing binaries, source, or a packaged app, maintainers should verify the applicable terms and attribution for each item, including:

- Ultralytics and YOLO/YOLO-World checkpoints and exports (including `.pt`, `.onnx`, and Core ML model artifacts).
- Python packages listed in `PyCharm/VisionGuide-Python/requirements.txt`, including Ultralytics, OpenCV, NumPy, PyTorch, Transformers, and edge-tts.
- Android Gradle dependencies, including AndroidX, CameraX, Compose, and ONNX Runtime Android.
- Apple platform frameworks.
- Research and screenshot images if they are added later; confirm ownership, licenses, and permissions for public sharing.

Keep a record of each artifact version, upstream source, license or terms, required notices, and redistribution decision. Do not represent this list as a completed legal review.

No project-wide open-source license file has been added. Until contributors choose and add one after confirming ownership and compatibility, reuse and redistribution permissions for project-authored material are not granted.
